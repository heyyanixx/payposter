How it works

  1. Type your UPI IDYour VPA, like name@okhdfcbank or 9xxxxxxxxx@ybl. Amount is optional.
  2. Pick a style & sizeStory (9:16) for WhatsApp/Instagram, Square, or A4 to print for a shop counter.
  3. Download or sharePNG, print to PDF, or send the pay link — the payer taps “Pay” and their UPI app opens with everything filled.
Is my data uploaded?

No. The poster, QR and link are made in your browser. There is no server and no tracking. Your last inputs are saved in this browser only (tap “Clear” to remove).

Does it work with every UPI app?

It uses the standard upi://pay format that UPI apps read. Some apps may ignore the pre-filled amount or note for personal (non-merchant) IDs — the payer can still type it.

Can I accept money with this like a business?

It's great for personal collections, small shops, fests and creators. For GST invoices, refunds and settlement reports you'd want a bank/merchant QR or a payment gateway.
